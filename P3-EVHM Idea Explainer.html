04
the idea — our contribution

Teach the network the laws of physics, then let it learn.

✗ A normal LSTM

Learn from data alone — and it cheerfully predicts the battery gaining capacity overnight. In our measured grid it broke physics in up to 73% of its steps and often never produced a life estimate at all.

✓ Our physics-informed LSTM

The training loss carries two laws from electrochemistry: capacity can never rise, and decay must follow an Arrhenius temperature rule. Measured effect: violations 0.366 → 0.076, accuracy up, on both machines.

Then a particle filter runs 5,000 simulated futures of that learned model — so instead of one number, sir gets a distribution: median life, plus the 95% range maintenance should plan around.

SAY "That is the novelty, sir. Others published physics-informed LSTMs without the filter, and LSTM-plus-filter papers without physics — one of them literally lists our physics constraints as future work. We are the first to put all four pieces — physics learning, filtering, GPU parallelism, and the benchmark — in one framework."
