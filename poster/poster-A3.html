THE PROBLEM / WHY BATTERY HEALTH IS HARD

A lithium-ion cell loses capacity with every charge cycle — slowly at first, then through a degradation knee that takes it below the 70–80% end-of-life threshold with almost no warning. A vehicle reports a charge percentage, not health, so the first sign of trouble is often the failure itself. What maintenance actually needs is a number with an error bar, enough cycles in advance to schedule the replacement.

Each ingredient of a solution exists in the literature — physics-informed training losses, LSTM × particle-filter hybrids, GPU-resident particle filters — but no published framework combines them on a real benchmark; the closest hybrid paper lists our physics constraints as future work. P3-EVHM fuses the four and measures what the fusion buys.

METHOD / WHAT THE MODEL IS TOLD NOT TO DO

A 1–32 LSTM learns capacity dynamics from a W = 8 cycle window, but its loss carries two laws of electrochemistry: the prediction may never claim the battery gained capacity, and its decline must respect an Arrhenius-style temperature prior fitted from the training cycles themselves.

(4) L = MSE(q̂, q) + λ1 · mean ReLU(q̂t − qt) + λ2 · mean(Δq̂t − g(Tt))²
(5) g(T) = clamp(Â · (T − T̄), min = 0)   —  a prior, not a hard clamp: violations fall ~5× but not to zero
(6–8) qi(k) = qi(k−1) − di + εk;   wi ∝ exp(−(qobs − qi)² / 2σ²);   RUL = median { k : qi(k) ≤ qEOL }

Prognosis is a distribution, not a point: the trained model seeds N simulated futures, each with its own decline rate, reweighted by the measured start-cycle capacity; the cycle at which a future crosses the EOL threshold is that future’s RUL. The model reports the median and the 95% interval — and the interval is calibrated, not decorative.

PARALLELISM / ONE CLOUD, ONE TENSOR OP

Futures are independent, so the particle cloud is embarrassingly parallel: each propagation step is a single vectorised tensor update over all N particles — data-parallel SIMT on the GPU, the identical NumPy code on the CPU. No multiprocessing, no multi-node.

At small N the warm CPU wins — we report that. The GPU pays off exactly where particle-filter prognostics needs it: large clouds for sharp intervals, in real time.

HONEST LIMITS / WHAT THIS IS NOT

Laboratory single-cell data — pack-level validity untestedd. Early prediction starts (40% of life, before the knee) can fail without the decline-prior floord. LSTM seed variance is material and reported. A research decision-support prototype — not a certified BMS safety function.
